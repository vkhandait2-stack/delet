Delete Your Account

This page explains how users of the Hanuman Lok Yatra app can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address registered with your Hanuman Lok Yatra account.
  2. Use the subject line Delete My Hanuman Lok Yatra Account.
  3. Include your registered account email address and, if applicable, your name.
  4. We may request reasonable information to verify that the request belongs to the account owner.

Account deletion request email:

vkhandait2@gmail.com

What will be deleted

After a valid request is verified, the user account and personal data associated with that account will be deleted, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required by law, to prevent fraud or abuse, to maintain security, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

Processing of requests

Account deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after verification.

Need help?

For questions about account deletion or privacy, contact: vkhandait2@gmail.com

Important: Deleting an account may permanently remove account-related information and may not be reversible.
